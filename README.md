🌟 ABOUT THE PROJECT

Pig Game is a fun and interactive turn-based dice game for two players.

Players take turns rolling a dice to collect points. Each roll adds points to their temporary score (Current).

Players can choose to Hold and save their accumulated points. However, if a player rolls a 1, they lose their temporary points for that turn, and the turn passes to the next player.

The first player to reach 50 points wins the game.

In this version, the game automatically ends as soon as a player's Current score reaches 50 or more.

The project features a modern black-and-gold design, smooth animations, and a responsive layout.

🎮 HOW TO PLAY
Player 1 starts the game.
Click Roll Dice to roll the dice.
Each roll adds points to the Current score.
Click Hold to save your points.
Rolling a 1 ends your turn.
The first player to reach 50 points wins.
